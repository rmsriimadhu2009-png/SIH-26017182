## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
*DETAILED EXPLANATION:

My idea is to create a digital marketplace called FarmDirect.FarmDirect is a digital marketplace that connects farmers and FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers. It provides logistics support and uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization. This helps farmers get better prices, consumers buy products at lower prices, and reduces supply chain inefficiencies

*HOW IT ADDRESSES THE PROBLEM:

In the traditional system, agricultural products pass through many middlemen before reaching consumers. This increases the price for customers and reduces the profit of farmers.
FarmDirect helps farmers sell their products directly to customers. It reduces unnecessary middlemen and transportation costs. It also helps farmers understand market demand and allows customers to buy fresh products at reasonable prices.

*INNOVATION AND UNIQUENESS:

The special feature of FarmDirect is that it combines direct selling, AI and delivery services in one platform.
- Farmers can sell products directly to customers.
- AI helps predict which products will be needed.
- The system finds better delivery routes.
- Customers can compare prices before buying.
- The website can support Tamil and other local languages.
This makes farming and selling easier for farmers and buying easier for customers

## Feasibility and Viability
*ANALYSIS OF THE FEASIBLITY OF THE IDEA:

This idea is possible to implement because the required technologies are available and affordable. A basic website can be developed using a laptop and free software. Initially, the project can be tested with a small group of farmers and customers. If it works well, it can be expanded to other areas

*POTENTIAL CHALLENGES AND RISKS:

Some farmers may not know how to use digital platforms. Internet facilities may not be available everywhere. Fruits and vegetables may get spoiled during transportation. Sometimes, the AI system may not predict the correct demand. Delivery costs may also be high for small orders

*STRATEGIES FOR OVERCOMING THESE CHALLENGES:

- Provide a simple website in local languages like Tamil.
- Help farmers register and use the platform.
- Combine nearby orders to reduce delivery costs.
- Use proper packaging to keep products fresh.
- Improve AI predictions using previous sales data.
- Start the project in a small area before expanding it.
